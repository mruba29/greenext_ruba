Frequently Asked Questions

Hiring Process & Candidate Guidance

Where are GreenNext engineering roles based?

We support roles across our regional focus hubs in Madurai, Coimbatore, Trichy, and Mangalore, offering flexible working arrangements depending on field and project requirements.

What backgrounds do you look for in candidates?

We welcome talent across electrical engineering, mechanical HVAC, computer science, network engineering, and data center facilities operations who share a commitment to technical precision.

How does the application and interview process work?

Submit your resume and background via our application form. Our technical team conducts an initial conversation, followed by an in-depth architectural and problem-solving discussion.
