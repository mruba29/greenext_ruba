Frequently Asked Questions

Banking & Financial Services Infrastructure Clarity

How does GreenNext support regulatory data localization and sovereignty requirements?

Our regional data center models keep all customer financial records, cryptographic keys, and transaction ledgers physically hosted within sovereign territory, satisfying national compliance mandates.

What recovery time objectives (RTO) are achievable with your disaster recovery designs?

With active-active synchronized architectures, RTO can approach near-zero, while active-passive automated warm standby configurations typically achieve recovery within minutes.

How is physical security enforced in banking co-location or private rooms?

We specify multi-factor biometric authentication, anti-tailgating mantrap entries, CCTV with behavioral analytics, and continuous perimeter monitoring.
