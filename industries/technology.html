Frequently Asked Questions

Technology Infrastructure Clarity

Why should a SaaS company consider private data center infrastructure over public cloud?

For stable, predictable workloads, private infrastructure typically offers 40% to 60% lower total cost of ownership compared to continuous cloud rental, while delivering superior I/O performance and avoiding noisy-neighbor lag.

How fast can a technology enterprise scale additional capacity with GreenNext?

Our modular facility designs feature pre-provisioned power busways and overhead fiber runs, allowing new server cabinets to be deployed and energized in days rather than months.

Does GreenNext support DevOps and infrastructure-as-code automation?

Yes. All facility telemetry and automated environmental controls expose open RESTful APIs that integrate cleanly with modern enterprise monitoring and observability stacks like Prometheus and Grafana.
